Terms of use

An educational model of the fourteen 8,000 m peaks: for understanding the mountains, never for finding your way on them.

Last updated %LAST_UPDATED%

Not for navigation or safety decisions

The terrain comes from a 30 m elevation model that rounds off sharp summits, so the 3D mountains stand up to about 250 m lower than their surveyed heights near the top; altitudes shown on the page are corrected to the documented ones. Route lines, camps and hazard zones are approximate reconstructions from published expedition accounts. Ice, rock and routes change from season to season. Do not use this site to plan a climb, to navigate, or to judge conditions or risk on a mountain.

Accuracy

Heights, first ascents, figures and history are taken from published sources and checked, but they can be wrong or out of date. If you find a mistake, please use “Suggest a correction” on the mountain’s page.
